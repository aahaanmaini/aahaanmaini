## Hi, I'm Aahaan!  

I'm a machine learning and research engineer studying Software Engineering at the University of Waterloo. At heart, I'm a builder who learns by shipping, breaking, and iterating, and the work I'm proudest of usually starts as a problem I can't stop thinking about, the kind that sits in the back of your mind, constantly turning over until the solution clicks.

Most of those problems sit downstream of the bitter lesson, the idea that the methods that win in the long run are the ones that scale with increased compute. A few are top of mind for me right now: how to build evals for unverifiable domains that you can actually steer a model with, what the right minimal scaffold for an agent looks like, and where test-time compute is best spent. I'm also drawn to post-training as the place where most of a model's usefulness gets shaped, and to designing products around models that get noticeably better every few months.
